StudyForge privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

StudyForge is a student project built for the Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon (October 2026). It is a study helper: you ask questions, upload notes, and take quizzes. This page explains, in plain English, what it does with your data.

Signing in

You can sign in with Google or press "Try demo". Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth. With Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture, and we use them only to sign you in and show who you are. "Try demo" creates an anonymous account with no personal data. We request only the email, profile and openid permissions from Google.

StudyForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we store

Your questions, the notes and images you upload, your solve history and your quiz results are stored in Supabase (a Postgres database and file storage), linked to your account.

Who else sees it

When you ask a question, the question (and any notes or images attached to it) is sent to an AI model hosted on Nebius Token Factory to generate the answer. The website is hosted on Vercel and the API on Render, so your requests pass through them. Python calculations run inside your own browser, not on our servers.

What we don't do

No ads. We do not sell or share your data for advertising. There are no analytics or tracking cookies. Supabase keeps your sign-in session in your browser's local storage, and the app saves a few small settings there too (such as your last quiz score).

Deleting your data

Email samarthkombli@gmail.com and ask us to delete your account and everything stored for it. An in-app "export my data" and "delete my account" panel is planned but not built yet.
